Your research team UNDER THE HOOD

SellKit quietly employs four specialist services. Their work shows up in the app already organized.
THE SCOUT · Exa Finds companies and spots the moment

Reads the public web — news, careers pages, announcements — to find companies that fit your profile and catch the events that make outreach timely: a new executive, a hiring spike, or efficiency pressure.

Why it matters: every radar card and queue target starts here. Without the scout, you'd be skimming press releases all morning.

THE RESEARCHER · Exa Agent Builds the account dossier

When you hit Refresh committee map, it reads up on one company and comes back with the five people who matter — buyer, champion, blocker, budget holder, technical evaluator — each with a reason and a source link.

Why it matters: an hour of account research compressed into a card you can verify, because every claim links to where it came from.

THE QUICK CHECKER · Parallel Tests your targeting quickly

Powers the ICP Preview Lab. Type a description of your ideal customer and it returns a fast sample — up to 10 matches with reasons — so you can see whether your criteria find the right companies.

Why it matters: you tune your aim on a small preview before committing to a broader list that may need a sharper definition.

THE VERIFIER · Apollo Confirms the work email

Once a target scores 75+ and you click Enrich work email, it verifies the buyer's business address so your message reaches a real inbox instead of bouncing.

Why it matters: email verification is deliberately gated, so SellKit only runs it on targets already worth contacting — and never fetches phone numbers or personal addresses.

One more, on the bench: Clay, a spreadsheet-style workspace for the occasional row that needs human judgment. You'll rarely meet it.

Four rules that never bend GUARDRAILS

The safety model stays visible without making you read every rule up front.
Nothing sends itself

No auto email, no LinkedIn automation, no Salesforce writes. Every outbound action is your click, in your tools.

Email enrichment is gated

Apollo runs only on a manual click, only at score 75+, only for work email, max 10 per batch.

Exports are safe to open

The CRM CSV neutralizes formula-looking cells, so provider-supplied text can't execute in Excel or Sheets.

Provider text is data, not orders

Research output is clamped and validated. It can inform a draft; it can never change the rules above.

If something looks off FIXES

Common states and what to do next.
Buttons like Track or Export are greyed out
Approve the draft first. Tracking and export unlock after approval, and each outcome can only be tracked once per target.
"Enrich work email" is blocked
The card states the reason: score under 75, already enriched, rejected/suppressed, or the 10-target batch cap has already been used.
The radar or ICP preview shows sample data
The provider was unavailable, so SellKit shows a labeled fallback. Try again later for live results.
The draft doesn't sound like me
Reopen onboarding, paste a real email into the voice question, and Save Progress. Drafts follow your example.
I'm stuck on the sign-in page
Sign out fully, reopen https://sellkit-mu.vercel.app, and use the approved account. You'll be redirected back after signing in.
