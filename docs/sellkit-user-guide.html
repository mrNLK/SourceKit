What SellKit Does

SellKit helps Mariah review account and buyer recommendations before outreach. It combines account discovery, buyer discovery, signal evidence, buying committee research, scoring, and draft copy into one review queue.

No automatic send. SellKit prepares drafts and exports. Mariah reviews and approves before anything is copied, downloaded, or imported elsewhere.

Before First Use

  • Use the approved work account to sign in.
  • Confirm that external sourcing and enrichment tools are allowed under company policy.
  • Keep provider keys server-side. Do not paste API keys into the browser or guide.
  • Use manual email, manual CRM import, and manual LinkedIn steps until Microsoft, Salesforce, or LinkedIn access is approved.

What to say

Use size, maturity, function, geography, and transformation context. For expert services, fit is usually about urgency plus complexity, not just industry.

Enterprise software or big-tech teams with 2,000+ employees, US first, active platform, AI, data, cloud, or product operating model initiatives. Avoid small startups unless they just hired a senior transformation leader.

Offer patterns

Change management consultants, former product/engineering executives, domain experts, or fractional operators who help teams make a difficult initiative real.

4 Buying signals Rank the events that make expert help timely, not merely interesting. v

High-intent triggers

New CTO/CIO/VP Eng, major reorg, AI platform launch, hiring spike for platform/data/product ops, cloud cost initiative, acquisition integration, enterprise transformation program, or public roadmap shift.

What good looks like

A visible initiative plus an owner. Example: "new VP Engineering hired in the last 90 days and the company is hiring platform productivity leaders."

Good SellKit input: "Strongest signals: new product/engineering leadership, AI/data platform rollout, change management after reorg, cloud or infra cost pressure, and senior hiring for platform/product ops."
5 Email voice Give SellKit a real tone to imitate: direct, credible, and tied to the signal. v

What to provide

One or two real emails, preferred subject length, whether to mention the signal, and how direct the ask should be.

Example opener

"Saw your platform org is hiring around developer productivity after the AI infra rollout. We help teams bring in former product and engineering operators who have led similar transitions."

Good SellKit input: "Short, plain, low-hype. Mention the specific signal in sentence one. Offer a useful expert angle, not a broad pitch. Ask for a quick compare-notes conversation."

Daily Workflow

  1. Sign in: open SellKit and authenticate with the approved account.
  2. Check provider stages: confirm the provider architecture matches the workflow: Exa for discovery, Parallel for fast lookup and preview, Exa Agent for dossiers, Apollo and Clay for selective enrichment.
  3. Review the queue: pick the highest-scored target and inspect the company, title, signal, and email confidence.
  4. Read evidence: open source links where needed. The score should be explainable from visible evidence.
  5. Check the buying committee: identify primary buyer, technical co-sponsor, and budget or ops sponsor.
  6. Approve only when ready: approve the draft after reviewing copy. Then copy/download the manual email and export CRM CSV if needed.

API Best Practices

Evidence-First Scoring

Every score component should be traceable. Store the provider, source URL, source title, source date, retrieval time, excerpt, confidence, and why the evidence matters.

  • Signal strength: link to a press release, hiring page, earnings note, product post, or funding source.
  • Title fit: link to the profile or public bio used to identify the buyer.
  • Company fit: link to Exa company metadata or the source supporting headcount, industry, funding, or tech stack.
  • Email confidence: show Apollo status separately from the score and keep raw enrichment output server-side.

Cost Controls

  • Debounce interactive searches and cache provider responses by normalized query.
  • Use Parallel Entity Search for quick lookups and FindAll Preview before full FindAll runs.
  • Cap Exa Agent, Exa Websets, and Parallel FindAll list sizes before running.
  • Use Apollo only after a target passes score thresholds. Bulk enrich in batches of 10.
  • Do not enrich phone numbers or personal emails unless explicitly approved.

Compliance Defaults

  • No automatic send. SellKit prepares copy, .eml files, and exports only after approval.
  • No automatic LinkedIn action. SellKit can prepare a note and profile link for manual use.
  • Keep all API keys server-side and never expose them in browser requests, screenshots, or exports.
  • Keep raw provider payloads separate from normalized app rows so data can be audited or deleted.
  • Use work email before personal email. Treat phone enrichment as disabled unless Mariah explicitly opts in.

Provider References

  • Exa Company Search - company discovery and structured company metadata.
  • Exa People Search - professional profiles and buyer discovery.
  • Exa Agent - deep research, grounded structured JSON, and account dossiers.
  • Exa Websets - verified async list-building and enrichment.
  • Parallel Entity Search - fast synchronous people and company lookup.
  • Parallel FindAll Preview - cheap validation before full FindAll runs.
  • Parallel FindAll - cited, verified, enriched list-building.
  • Apollo People Enrichment - verified contact enrichment after scoring.
  • Clay HTTP API and Clay Webhooks - manual enrichment workspace and row handoff.

Troubleshooting

  • Login loop: sign out, reopen the SellKit URL, and use the approved account.
  • Copy is disabled: approve the draft first. Copy/download stays disabled before approval.
  • CRM export is not ready: approve at least one target.
  • Draft sounds wrong: update the onboarding email voice field with a real example.
  • Score seems unclear: inspect Evidence-First Scoring and source links before approving.
