SourceKit Pilot Guide

Download PDFOpen SourceKit

1Sign in

Go to getsourcekit.vercel.app and sign in with your invited email, using Google or a password.

Why it matters

Pilot access is invite-only.

2Paste a job description

In New Search, choose Job Description, paste the text or a posting link, and click Build Sourcing Strategy.

Why it matters

More detail gives a sharper strategy. The build takes 30-60 seconds.

3Review the strategy, then search

Edit the four parts below, then click Search with this strategy.

Why it matters

Every entry is a suggestion; quick edits steer the whole search.

The four parts of a strategy

PartWhat it showsYou can
Target repositoriesOpen-source projects where the right engineers work.Remove or add repos.
CompaniesTeams likely to have the talent, tagged Competitor, Adjacent or Talent Hub.Retag or add companies.
SkillsMust Have (blue) and Nice to Have (gray). Must-haves weigh most.Add or remove skills.
EEA signalsEvidence of Exceptional Ability (EEA): papers, competition results, notable open-source work.Switch off any you do not need.

4Scan results and Hidden Gems

Candidates are ranked by fit score (0-100), each with a one-line reason. Click Hidden Gems to filter.

Why it matters

It surfaces strong matches with a smaller public profile (high score, few followers).

5Add to your pipeline

Open a profile and click Add to Pipeline. The candidate appears under Sourced in the Pipeline tab.

Why it matters

Track each person from Sourced to Contacted, Recruiter Screen and Moved to ATS.

FAQ

How long does a search take?
Under a minute for the strategy, a few minutes for the search. You can switch tabs meanwhile.
What are the daily limits?
Per account, per day: 25 searches, 150 enrichments (email lookups, profile details) and 100 AI calls (strategies, drafts, Bulk Actions). Limits reset at midnight UTC (5pm PT, 4pm PT after Nov 1).
How do I get the best results?
Paste the full job description, start with Hidden Gems, and find emails only for your shortlist.
Why do I not see founders or executives?
Ungettable profiles (founders, executives, board members, very famous engineers) are hidden by default. Click Show Ungettable to include them.
Who do I contact for help?
Reply to Mike Rubino with access issues, questions or feedback.
