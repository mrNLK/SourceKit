SourceKit Pilot Guide

Quick start for AI Fund portfolio CEOs. Michael Rubino, AI Fund, 2026-10-16.

SourceKit turns a job description into a sourcing strategy, then finds engineers and scores each one 0-100 on their actual open-source work. The pilot is free and invite-only; a first session takes about 10 minutes.

QUICK START

  1. Sign in at getsourcekit.vercel.app with the email your invitation was sent to.
  2. Paste a job description in New Search and click Build Sourcing Strategy.
  3. Check the strategy, then click Search with this strategy.
  4. Turn on Hidden Gems and scan the scored candidates.
  5. Open a candidate and click Add to Pipeline.

STEP BY STEP

1. Sign in

Go to getsourcekit.vercel.app and sign in with Continue with Google or email and password, using the address your invitation was sent to.

Why it matters: The pilot is invite-only, so other addresses are not let in.

2. Paste a job description

In New Search, choose Job Description, paste the full text or a posting link, and click Build Sourcing Strategy.

Why it matters: More detail on role, team and must-haves gives a sharper strategy and better scores; the build takes 30-60 seconds.

3. Check the strategy, then search

Edit any of the four parts below, then click Search with this strategy at the bottom of the page.

Why it matters: Every entry is a suggestion, and a minute of edits here steers the whole search.

The four parts of a strategy

PartWhat it showsWhat you can do
Target repositoriesOpen-source projects where the right engineers work, each with a one-line reason.Remove off-target repos or add your own.
CompaniesTeams likely to have the talent, tagged Competitor, Adjacent or Talent Hub.Change a tag or add a company.
SkillsMust Have (blue chips) and Nice to Have (gray chips). Must-haves weigh most in scoring.Add or remove skills.
EEA signalsEvidence of Exceptional Ability, such as owning a core project or published technical work.Switch off any you do not need.

4. Scan results and Hidden Gems

Candidates are ranked by a 0-100 fit score with a one-line reason; click Hidden Gems to see only strong engineers with a low public profile.

Why it matters: Hidden gems hear from recruiters less often, so they are more likely to reply.

5. Add to your pipeline

Click a name to open the profile, then click Add to Pipeline. The candidate lands in the Sourced column of the Pipeline tab.

Why it matters: The board tracks each person from Sourced to Contacted, Recruiter Screen and Moved to ATS.

FAQ

How long does a search take?
A strategy takes under a minute and a full search a couple of minutes. You can switch tabs while it runs.
What are the daily limits?
Per account: 25 searches, 150 enrichments and 100 AI calls a day. Enrichments cover email lookups and extra profile details; AI calls cover strategy building, outreach drafts and Bulk Actions. Limits reset at midnight UTC (5 pm PT).
How do I get the best results?
Paste the full job description, start with Hidden Gems, and find emails only for your shortlist so enrichments last.
Why do I not see founders or executives?
Ungettable candidates (founders, executives, board members and very famous engineers) are hidden by default. Click Show Ungettable to include them.
